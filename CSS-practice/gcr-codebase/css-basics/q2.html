Jaipur - The Pink City

History Culture Travel

History

Jaipur is a historic city known for its forts, palaces, markets and planned streets. It is the capital of Rajasthan.

Culture

Jaipur is famous for colorful festivals, traditional crafts, local food, music and Rajasthani hospitality.

Places to Visit

Amber Fort, City Palace, Hawa Mahal and Jantar Mantar are popular attractions in Jaipur.

Why Visit Jaipur?

Jaipur offers history, architecture, shopping, food and beautiful cultural experiences.
